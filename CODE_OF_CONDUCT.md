# Code of conduct

Health Journal is a small project about a sensitive subject: people's health. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to keep it a respectful place.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep it on topic and constructive. Say what you see and what you propose.
- Respect privacy. Do not post your own or anyone else's health data, names or screenshots with real entries in an issue. Use made-up values.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes in ([Governance](GOVERNANCE.md)). A "no" is about the project's scope, not about you.

## Health topics

People here may live with a condition. Do not give medical advice to each other in the project's spaces and do not ask for it; the app itself records and shows what you enter and assesses nothing ([ADR 0021](docs/adr/0021-medication-compliance-and-privacy.md)).

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer privately through the repository's **Security** tab (choose private reporting and say it is a conduct matter), or by a direct message on GitHub to the repository owner. Reports are treated in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules, after a warning where that makes sense.
