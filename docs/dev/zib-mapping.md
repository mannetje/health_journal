# zib mapping

The Dutch health information standards (zibs, *zorginformatiebouwstenen*, maintained by Nictiz) give shared names and value sets for health concepts. The app uses them as a **naming and value-set guide only**: it makes no claim of conformance and does not exchange data with care systems (see [ADR 0026](../adr/0026-oxygen-saturation-without-assessment.md) and the `compliance` capability). Back to the [developer docs](README.md).

> **Status: mostly checked.** The oxygen saturation and fluid rows were read in full on 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-14. The pulse, blood pressure, weight, glucose, migraine, sleep, energy, body temperature and activity rows were checked against the zib pages on zibs.nl on 2026-11-16, at the 2026-1 pre-publication (the versions named in each row). Rows still marked *to verify* have not been read against a zib. Rows marked *own* have no zib counterpart. A zib version number here is the version read, not a claim that nothing newer exists.

## How to use this page
When a new metric or field is designed, look up its zib on [zibs.nl](https://zibs.nl), compare its concept names, units and value sets with the plan, and add a row here. Follow the zib where it costs nothing and adds no function; say here where and why the app differs.

## Mapping

Rows marked *proposed, not built* describe an open OpenSpec change under `openspec/changes/`; nothing of them exists in the app yet. Every other row is a shipped feature.
