## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mindfulness text in English and Dutch
Every label, description, exercise phase name, spoken cue, safety note and message of the Mindfulness area SHALL exist in English and Dutch and follow the app language. Dutch text SHALL fit narrow phones without clipping.

#### Scenario: Dutch phase names
- **WHEN** the app language is Dutch and an exercise runs
- **THEN** the phase names and the spoken cues are Dutch

#### Scenario: Language change
- **WHEN** the language is changed while the area is closed
- **THEN** the next opening shows the new language
